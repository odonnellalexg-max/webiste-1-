Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve Waunakee, DeForest, Lodi, Verona, Middleton, Madison and Fitchburg, Wisconsin, and we consider larger projects farther out.

Are you licensed and insured?

We hold a 3.0 Turf & Landscape Commercial Applicator certification and carry business insurance. Documentation is available on request.

How do free quotes work?

Send a request or call us. We review the details, visit the property when needed, and send a written quote.

Do you offer yearly contracts for commercial properties and HOAs?

Yes. Yearly contracts cover maintenance and snow removal, with a written scope and pre-season pricing for snow.

How does snow removal work?

Service is triggered by snowfall. Your agreement sets the trigger depth, and we plow and treat with salt or de-icer.

What materials do you use for patios and walls?

Patios: pavers, natural stone and stamped concrete. Walls: natural stone, segmental block and timber.

How long does a design-build project take?

It depends on size and scope. Your written quote includes a schedule.

Do you handle fertilizer and weed treatments?

Yes. Applications are handled by our certified commercial applicator.

Can you work with our HOA board?

Yes. We write board-ready proposals and can attend a meeting on request.
